MASTER STATISTICAL INTERPRETATION
---------------------------------
Across 65 published Turkish coffee samples, OTA was detected in 53 samples (81.5%), while 12 samples were below the 0.23 µg/kg detection limit. Detection prevalence did not differ significantly across 2021–2024. The primary full-data censored model found no significant overall year effect on OTA concentration, and an independent nonparametric cross-check likewise found no significant year-associated distributional difference. Some detected-only exploratory analyses showed year-pair differences, but these should not be interpreted as evidence of a demonstrated temporal increase.

REGULATORY SCREENING
---------------------
At a 3.0 µg/kg screening threshold, 3 samples exceeded the threshold (2021_02, 2022_11 and 2022_15). At 5.0 µg/kg, one sample exceeded the threshold (2022_11, 19.11 µg/kg). Threshold interpretation depends on the applicable product category and regulation and should not be generalized beyond the relevant regulatory context.

LIMITATIONS
-----------
1. The published OTA dataset is not sequence-paired.
2. The concentration analysis therefore cannot establish a sequence-to-OTA quantitative relationship.
3. Below-LOD observations are left-censored and their exact concentrations are not known.
4. The detected-only analyses are conditional on OTA detection and should not replace the full censored analysis.
5. The dataset contains only four sampling years and therefore does not establish a long-term temporal trend.
6. The 19.11 µg/kg observation is an extreme detected value; sensitivity analyses were therefore performed.
7. The computational BLAST pipeline developed separately in this project is a pre-screen/triage tool and does not prove OTA toxin production.
